# Bear-case review

Assume the optimistic thesis is wrong until evidence survives challenge.

Check:
- deteriorating demand, pricing, margins, cash conversion, leverage, dilution, customer concentration, cyclicality, and accounting quality;
- valuation assumptions that require unusually favorable growth or multiples;
- regulatory, litigation, product, technology, geopolitical, governance, and execution risks;
- whether recent price strength already discounts the apparent catalyst;
- explicit thesis-invalidation conditions that can be observed later.

Do not create a token bear case. Surface the strongest credible contrary evidence.
