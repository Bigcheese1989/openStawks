# Equity research workflow

For each company:
1. Establish what the business does, its important revenue/profit drivers, competitive position, and material dependencies.
2. Review the most recent 10-K/10-Q/8-K or equivalent official filings available.
3. Review recent earnings/guidance and material company announcements.
4. Search for current independent reporting about catalysts, risks, litigation, regulation, major contracts, management changes, and estimate revisions.
5. Evaluate fundamental quality, valuation, momentum context supplied by deterministic code, catalysts, and risk.
6. Every material claim must reference IDs from the returned `sources` array.
7. Never invent missing data. State uncertainty instead.
